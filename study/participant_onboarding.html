PostureGuard — Participant Onboarding & Consent

A study on smartphone-based posture feedback · [INSTITUTION / DEPARTMENT]

1About this study

What is being tested. PostureGuard is a research prototype for Android that monitors sitting posture while you use your phone. It uses the front-facing camera together with the phone's motion sensor to estimate your head and shoulder position, and gives you feedback when your posture degrades.

What we want to find out. We are comparing two different ways of giving that feedback, to learn which one people find more effective and less disruptive:

  • Version A — screen dimming. Your screen gradually darkens when your posture slips, and brightens again when you correct it.
  • Version B — posture guide. A faint outline of your own upright posture appears on screen as a reference to match.

You will use both versions, one after the other, in an order we assign to you. This lets us compare the two on the same person rather than across different people, which is far more reliable. Both versions measure posture in exactly the same way — only the feedback differs.

What taking part involves.

  • Install the PostureGuard app on your own Android phone (Android 5.0 or newer, front camera required).
  • Enter the participant ID shown to you at the end of this form.
  • Do a one-time calibration: sit in a comfortable, upright posture for about 5 seconds while the app records your personal baseline.
  • Phase 1: use the app with the first version during your normal phone use for [PHASE LENGTH], with sessions of roughly [SESSION DURATION].
  • A short break of [WASHOUT PERIOD] with no app use at all.
  • Phase 2: the same again with the second version. We will tell you when and how to switch.
  • Optionally complete a short follow-up questionnaire at the end.

Total commitment is roughly [TOTAL DURATION]. Which version you start with is decided by us, not chosen — this is deliberate, and it keeps the comparison fair.

Possible risks and discomforts. The app dims your screen or draws an overlay when it detects poor posture, which some people find distracting. Running the camera continuously uses battery and can make the phone warm. There is no physical risk beyond ordinary phone use. You are free to stop a session, uninstall the app, or leave the study at any point.

Benefits. There is no direct benefit to you, and [COMPENSATION, or: no compensation is offered]. PostureGuard is a research prototype, not a medical device. It cannot diagnose or treat any condition. If you have persistent neck or back pain, please consult a healthcare professional rather than relying on this app.

2What data is collected, and what is not

The camera runs during every session, but no image ever leaves your phone. Camera frames are analysed on your device, in memory, and discarded immediately. No photo, video, or skeleton image is saved to your phone or uploaded to us. We never see your face, your room, or anyone else in the frame.

Sent to our research server

Once per session, after the session ends — never live.

user_id The random participant ID generated at the end of this form.
session_id A random identifier for one monitoring session.
timestamp The time of each posture reading (roughly one per second).
score A posture quality number from 0 to 100.
zone Which quality band that score falls into (good / warning / bad).
streak How many seconds of good posture you held in a row.
battery Your phone's battery level, to measure the app's power cost.
temperature Your phone's temperature, to check the app does not overheat it.
app_version Which build of the app produced the data.

Each reading also records which of the two versions produced it, so that the two can be compared. Your answers on this form, your assigned order, and your phone model are stored alongside these, linked by user_id.

Never collected

  • Photos, video, or camera frames of any kind
  • Skeleton or landmark coordinates
  • Audio or microphone data
  • Location or GPS data
  • Your contacts, messages, browsing, or which other apps you use
  • Any account credentials — the app has no login

How your identity is protected

Your data is pseudonymised, not fully anonymous, and we want to be precise about the difference. Everything sent from the app carries only your random participant ID. However, we keep a separate, access-restricted list linking your name to that ID. We keep it for one reason: so that if you later ask us to delete your data, we can actually find it. Nobody outside the research team can see this list, and it is stored separately from the study data.

Anything we publish or share — papers, presentations, datasets — contains only aggregated results or ID-labelled records. Your name never appears.

Your right to withdraw

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time, without giving a reason and without any disadvantage to you. To withdraw, email [CONTACT EMAIL] and quote your participant ID — you do not need to explain why. Your data will be deleted, unless it has already been merged into an aggregated analysis that cannot be separated, in which case we will tell you honestly what could and could not be removed. You also have the right to ask what data we hold about you and to have errors corrected.
